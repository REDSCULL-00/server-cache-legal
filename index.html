Server Cache — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlascentum ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you play Server Cache or interact with us in connection with the game. Atlascentum is a sole-trader business operated by Mathew, registered in Australia.

Information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to run the game and support players:

  • Account & platform data — your Epic Games account display name and ID, used to identify you to other players in multiplayer sessions.
  • Gameplay data — in-game progress, saves, and session data, stored to let you continue your game and play with others.
  • Technical data — device type, operating system, IP address, and crash/error logs, used to keep the game running and to diagnose bugs.
  • Purchase data — if you buy the game or any in-game content, the transaction is processed by Epic Games; we do not see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.).
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us directly (e.g. a support email), kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

How we use this information

  • To operate core features: multiplayer sessions, save data, matchmaking.
  • To fix bugs and improve performance and stability.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations as an Australian business.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Third parties

Server Cache is distributed through the Epic Games Store and may use Epic Online Services for multiplayer, sign-in, and purchases. Epic's own handling of your data is governed by Epic's Privacy Policy. We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

Data retention

We keep gameplay and account-linked data for as long as you actively play the game, and support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request. Technical/crash logs are retained for a limited period for debugging purposes and then deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Server Cache is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age set by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
