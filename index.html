Server Cache — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlascentum ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses information when you play Server Cache or interact with us in connection with the game. Atlascentum is a sole-trader business operated by Mathew, registered in Australia.

What Server Cache does not have

Server Cache has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics tracking, and no in-game chat.

Information we collect

  • Display name — the name you type in-game. Other players in your session see this name; we don't verify or link it to any real identity.
  • Crash and error reports — when the game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to us through Unity's Diagnostics service. It contains a random ID generated for your installation, your PC's operating system, hardware and performance information, and the error itself. It never contains your name. Unity keeps this report for 90 days. A copy of each crash report also stays on your own PC, in the game's install folder (CrashReports).
  • Online play connection data — online play uses Unity Gaming Services. Each time you start the game you're given a new anonymous player ID, and your IP address is used only to connect you to the other players in your session.
  • Feedback you submit — if you use the in-game feedback feature, the text you write, the display name and game version shown with it, are sent to a small server we run, which forwards it to a private channel we read. That server also records the sending IP address briefly, only to limit how often the same sender can submit feedback (spam prevention) — it does not otherwise store or use your IP.
  • Purchase data — if you buy the game or any in-game content, the transaction is processed by Epic Games; we do not see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.).
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us directly (e.g. a support email), kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

How we use this information

  • To let you play online with other people (connecting sessions, showing display names).
  • To find, understand and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To read and act on feedback you choose to send us.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations as an Australian business.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third parties

Server Cache is distributed through the Epic Games Store and uses Unity Technologies services (Diagnostics, Gaming Services) as described above. Epic's handling of your data is governed by Epic's Privacy Policy, and Unity's by Unity's Privacy Policy. We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

Data retention

Crash reports are kept by Unity for 90 days. Feedback messages are kept only as long as useful for development. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. We don't otherwise hold a persistent record tied to you — there is no account to delete.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Server Cache is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age set by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
